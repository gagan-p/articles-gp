# Fact-check: Indian software essay company claims (verified 2026-08-20)

## 1. MapMyIndia / C.E. Info Systems
- C.E. Info Systems (C.E. Infosystems Pvt. Ltd., 1995–2021) = MapmyIndia. Founded 1995, New Delhi, by Rakesh & Rashmi Verma. NSE: MAPMYINDIA, BSE: 543425.
- IPO listing: **21 December 2021** on BSE & NSE (listed ₹1,581 on BSE, +53.05% over ₹1,033 issue price; ₹1,565 on NSE, +51.5%). The "Dec 2024" dating is FALSE — Dec 2024 was the Mappls consumer-business separation governance controversy, not an IPO.
- Consumer brand/app: **Mappls** (confirmed, e.g. ET 18 Sep 2023 interview with MapmyIndia MD).
- "MapMyIndia (now KOO)" is **FALSE**. Koo was an unrelated microblogging app by Bombinate Technologies (Bengaluru), founders Aprameya Radhakrishna & Mayank Bidawatka; founded Nov 2019, launched 2020, dissolved 3 July 2024.

## 2. AdventNet / Zoho
- Founded 1996 as AdventNet, Inc. in **New Jersey, US** (Sridhar Vembu & Tony Thomas). Reviewer correct; essay's "Chennai, India" founding is FALSE (Chennai is current HQ).
- ManageEngine division started **2002** (reviewer correct; essay's 2005 wrong).
- Zoho CRM and Zoho Writer (first cloud/office products) released **2005** — TRUE.
- Renamed **Zoho Corporation in 2009** — TRUE.

## 3. Zoho user milestones
- 50M users (2020): NOT VERIFIED and contradicted — Zoho's own recap (ToI/BusinessWire, Sept 2023): 1M users (2008) → 100M (2023), "last 50 million added within the past five years" → 50M mark ~2018.
- 100M users: **5–6 September 2023** (first bootstrapped SaaS to reach it; $1B revenue in FY22).
- Latest: **150M+ users and 1M paying customers**, announced 18 Feb 2026 (30th anniversary). "130M" not supported.

## 4. Zoho "own OS" (July 2023)
- ToI 21 Jul 2023: "Zoho will launch OS for laptops in a year" — **future/announced**, NOT "built". Linux-based **laptop** OS ("work on which is on"); ET CIO 22 Jul 2023: "working on building its own OS for laptops" + server systems to go live within 5 years. Essay wording "built its own OS" is overstated/false.

## 5. YugabyteDB
- Founded **February 2016**, Sunnyvale CA, by ex-Facebook engineers Kannan Muthukkaruppan, Karthik Ranganathan, Mikhail Bautin (Yugabyte, Inc.). Launched from stealth Nov 2017 with $8M Series A (Lightspeed). **US-founded**; no evidence of Indian incorporation — "re-domiciled to US" is FALSE.

## 6. MinIO
- MinIO, Inc. founded **November 2014** by Anand Babu (AB) Periasamy, Harshavardhana, Frederick Kautz & Garima Kapoor. **US company** (Silicon Valley). India-founded claim FALSE (founder is Indian-origin, ex-Gluster).

## 7. Hasura
- Founded **2017** by Tanmai Gopal & Rajoshi Ghosh (Bangalore origin); GraphQL Engine open-sourced **11 July 2018** (PRWeb launch release, dateline "San Francisco and Bangalore"); license **Apache-2.0** (GitHub repo created 18 Jun 2018). Became "India's first open-source unicorn" Feb 2022 ($100M Series C, $1B valuation). Genuine India-origin → US-headquartered case — TRUE (nuance: dual SF+Bangalore HQ from the 2018 open-source launch itself).

## 8. Ola Maps
- **July 2024** full cutover from Google Maps to in-house Ola Maps (after May 2024 Azure→Krutrim cloud exit). Bhavish Aggarwal X post 5 Jul 2024: "We used to spend ₹100 cr a year but we've made that 0 this month by moving completely to our in house Ola maps!" — ~₹100 crore/year saving claim TRUE (as claimed by CEO).
- Built on **OpenStreetMap** (attributed in-app) + proprietary Ola data (own fleet). TRUE.
- Context: Ola acquired Pune geospatial startup GeoSpoc (Oct 2021); Ola Cabs rebranded Ola Consumer Aug 2024.
